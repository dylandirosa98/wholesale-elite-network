GOOD QUESTIONS

How does
the team work?

Do I need all four agents?

No. Pick one, two, three, or all four. Every combination at the same team size has the monthly price shown above; third-party usage and data access are separate.

What is included in the price?

The monthly service price covers the agreed agent workflows and shared deal view for one business. Setup is $1,000 one time. Your GHL account, carrier/message usage, licensed property data, and other third-party charges are separate. We set workflow capacity and any custom-integration scope before activation.

Can the Underwriting Agent tell me the exact right offer?

No. It would show source-linked comps, assumptions, and a range for review. Property condition, missing data, local context, title, and deal costs still need human verification.

How does the Dispositions Agent learn my buyers?

Its scoped workflow uses buyer criteria and interaction history that you are authorized to use—such as location, price range, property type, and prior feedback—to prepare a reviewable shortlist. It does not invent preferences or guarantee a buyer.

Can the agents send or negotiate without me?

No. A human must explicitly approve each external send or a defined campaign batch; suppression and quiet-hours checks still apply. The Dispositions Agent may suggest counteroffers, but a human approves material price, terms, and commitments. Broad standing permission is not part of the current pilot.

Would the Transaction Coordinator handle legal paperwork?

Its scope is tracking milestones, document status, owners, and reminders—not providing legal advice or approving contracts, signatures, or closing funds. Those decisions remain with the responsible people and professionals.

Where would messages be sent from?

After go-live gates pass, any approved sends would flow through the client's GHL subaccount and client-owned number, using the client's Telnyx connection. Agents would not send directly through Telnyx or from a Fee Finders or Wholesale AI number.

Can this integrate with any CRM?

Yes. We can connect the agent workflow to your existing CRM, subject to the access and integration options your platform provides. We confirm the connection method, data permissions, and any custom work during onboarding. Approved external messages still run through your client-owned GHL subaccount and number, with human approval and compliance checks.

How do I message and assign my agents?

During onboarding, we configure a supported messaging app you prefer, such as Telegram or iMessage, so you can message your agents and assign work from your phone or desktop. Channel availability depends on your accounts and devices and is confirmed before activation. Messages to leads and buyers are separate: they still need approval and run through your client-owned GHL subaccount.

When can my agents start?

After your scope, data access, approval controls, and sandbox tests are agreed and the go-live checks pass. These are published package prices, not a promise of immediate activation or a working checkout.
